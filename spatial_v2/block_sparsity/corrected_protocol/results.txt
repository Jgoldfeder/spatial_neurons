ViT-base / CIFAR-100 block pruning, CORRECTED PROTOCOL — final results (2026-08-02)
====================================================================================

Setup: vit_base_patch16_224 (ImageNet-pretrained), finetuned 4ep on CIFAR-100 -> dense 91.1.
16x16 weight blocks over all Linear layers. Iterative gradual pruning over targets
[20,40,55,65,75,80,85,90,95,97,98,99]% blocks removed; after each cut, 6 epochs x 50k
(full train set) finetune, AdamW lr 5e-5, cosine per stage. "reorder" tiling from
swap-assignment positions (function-preserving: verified bit-identical, 91.10 at every step).
Spatial arms: 6 pre-organization epochs (dense, wiring cost gamma=256, re-swap per epoch)
BEFORE the first cut, wiring cost kept on during stage finetunes.

Arms:
  taylor       = taylor block scoring ((g*W)^2 block-summed), no spatial cost, no pre-org
  taylorscore  = spatial pre-org + wiring cost + TAYLOR scoring     <- BEST METHOD >=75%
  regular      = spatial pre-org + wiring cost + magnitude scoring (run 1; grid ended at 90)
  polish       = regular, but gamma off for last 2 finetune epochs of each stage

Key findings
------------
1. spatial pre-org + taylor scoring wins every stage from 75% to 99%; its margin over
   taylor at IDENTICAL selection+budget (95-99%: +0.7,+4.4,+4.8,+4.2) is the cleanly
   attributed contribution of spatial organization.
2. taylor wins <=55% by ~2 pts = residual tax of the wiring cost (gamma=256 costs ~5-7 pts
   dense, partially refunded during masked finetuning).
3. gamma-off "polish" adds nothing (<=90%, within noise) and is the worst arm at 95-99%:
   keep the wiring cost on throughout.
4. Run-to-run noise (same seed, cuDNN nondeterminism; taylor run1 vs rerun): up to
   +/-2.5 pts at 75-90%. Margins below ~2.5 pts in that region are not interpretable.

Why the original suite (parent dir: iter_vit.py + vit_suite_orchestrator.sh) disagreed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original suite had spatial@90 = 29.4, taylor@90 = 58.0. Two protocol flaws:
 (a) no pre-organization: the first (20%) cut hit the never-organized base, scored by
     block-L2 magnitude -> instant collapse to chance (0.77%); everything after descends
     from a rebuilt-from-rubble model. Pre-org at sufficient dose makes the same cut FREE
     (84.4 post-cut vs 84.2 dense). Weak dose does NOT work (2ep@gamma=64 -> 1.48%).
 (b) starved finetunes (2ep x 20k per stage): endpoint at 20% is mostly budget
     (blind mag cut rebuilds 0.77 -> 83.3 short budget -> ~87 long budget; taylor@90
     gains +20 pts from budget alone).

Known gaps before publication: random-mask control (at 85-99% every cut lands near chance
and finetune does the heavy lifting), 2nd seed (75-90% margins vs noise), gamma sweep,
taylor gradient estimate uses only ~11 minibatches, no KD, spatial arms get 6 extra
epochs total (pre-org) vs taylor.
